Privacy Policy

Plain-language explanation of what this site stores and what it doesn't.

Last updated: . This page is maintained by Pipline Forex Academy to answer common privacy questions about this site. It is not a legal certification.

Summary

This is a static educational site. There are no user accounts. Your quiz scores, lesson progress, saved items and theme preference are stored in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device. Anything you type into the contact or newsletter forms is also held in that same local storage in this build.

What is stored in your browser

  • Theme preference — whether you chose light or dark.
  • Lesson progress — which lessons you marked complete.
  • Saved items — lessons, patterns and articles you bookmarked.
  • Quiz best score — your highest quiz result.
  • Form entries — contact and newsletter submissions made on this device.

You can erase all of it at any time using the button below, or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

What we collect

This build sets no advertising cookies, runs no analytics scripts and does not build a profile of you. The only third-party request the pages make is to Google Fonts to load typefaces; your browser's IP address is visible to that service as part of any normal web request.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Because form data stays on your device in this build, there is nothing for us to share.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data held about you. Since the data described here is stored locally on your device, you can exercise all of those directly by clearing it. If you have contacted us by email and want that correspondence removed, write to us and say so.

Children

This site is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of majority in their jurisdiction.

Security

Because nothing sensitive is transmitted or stored on a server, the main security consideration is your own device. If you use a shared computer, clear site data when you're finished.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated. Material changes will be noted on the home page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.

Educational disclaimer. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Trading forex on margin carries a high risk of loss.
