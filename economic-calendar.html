How to trade around news

  • Spreads widen and slippage spikes in the seconds around a high-impact release. Your stop may fill far past its price.
  • The market prices the forecast in advance. The move comes from the surprise, and often reverses within minutes.
  • Beginners should be flat before high-impact events, not positioned for them.
  • A workable approach is waiting for the first fifteen-minute candle to close and trading the retest of its range.
  • Central bank press conferences move price more than the decision they follow.

Reading impact ratings

High-impact events routinely move majors 60–120 pips within an hour. Medium-impact releases produce 20–50 pips and are usually tradeable with normal risk. Low-impact items rarely justify a change of plan but can amplify an existing move if they line up with the same story.

The real signal is the gap between actual and forecast, weighted by how surprised the market is — a small miss on a closely-watched inflation print outweighs a large miss on a secondary survey.
