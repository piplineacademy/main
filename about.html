About Pipline Forex Academy

Free structured education for people who want to learn trading properly rather than quickly.

Why this exists

Most forex education online is either a sales funnel or a firehose of disconnected videos. Neither produces a trader. What does is a sequence: understand the mechanics, learn to read a chart, get the risk maths right, then spend a long time practising with real feedback.

This academy is that sequence, written down, in order, for free. There is no signal group, no mentorship upsell and no broker referral behind any of it.

How the curriculum works

Thirteen sections, designed to be worked through once in order and then used as reference. Lessons and patterns can be marked complete or saved, and everything is stored locally in your browser — there's no account and we don't collect your progress.

What we deliberately don't do

  • No signals. Copying entries teaches you nothing and stops working the moment the source does.
  • No performance claims. Anyone promising a monthly return figure is describing marketing, not trading.
  • No broker recommendations. Regulation and conditions vary by country; that's a decision to research locally.
  • No shortcuts. The uncomfortable truth is that competence takes a long time, and every method that skips the work also skips the skill.

Who this is for

Complete beginners who want a structured path, and self-taught traders with gaps — usually in position sizing and process — who want to fill them systematically.

Our teaching principles

Plain language over jargon. Mechanics before indicators. Risk before entries. Process before profit. Every claim explained rather than asserted.

Risk disclosure

Trading foreign exchange on margin carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. The high degree of leverage can work against you as well as for you. You could sustain a loss of some or all of your initial investment. Everything on this site is educational content and general information only — it is not financial, investment or tax advice, and no part of it is a recommendation to trade any instrument.
