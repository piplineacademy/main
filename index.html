Common beginner questions

Enough that a 1% risk is a meaningful position but small enough that losing it changes nothing in your life. Most people start on a demo account for at least eight weeks, then fund a small live account. The skill you're buying with that money is emotional data, not returns.
There is no honest number, but the pattern is consistent: months of study, then a long stretch of small, controlled losses while your process stabilises. Anyone who gives you a fixed timeline is selling something.
No. Indicators summarise price you can already see. They are useful for consistency and for filtering, not for prediction. Learn to read raw price first, then add at most two tools.
Position sizing. Not entries. A trader with mediocre entries and strict 0.5% risk survives long enough to improve; a trader with excellent entries and 10% risk does not survive one bad week.
